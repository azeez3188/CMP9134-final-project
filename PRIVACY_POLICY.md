# Privacy Policy – Robot Management System

## What PII is Collected?
This system collects the following Personally Identifiable Information (PII) 
as part of its Mission Logging (Audit Trail) feature: usernames of authenticated 
users, IP addresses of connecting clients, and timestamps of all commands issued 
to the robot fleet.

## Why is it Collected?
This data is collected solely to satisfy the safety audit requirements of the 
Ground Control Station. Audit logs are essential for accountability, incident 
investigation, and compliance with operational safety standards. No data is 
collected beyond what is strictly necessary (Data Minimisation principle).

## How is it Secured and Retained?
Access to audit logs is restricted exclusively to users assigned the 'Auditor' 
role via Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). Logs are retained for a maximum of 
90 days, after which they are automatically purged in accordance with the 
Storage Limitation principle of UK GDPR. All log data is stored encrypted at 
rest and transmitted over HTTPS.
